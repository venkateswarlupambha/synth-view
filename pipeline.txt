        |
        v
Step 2: capture/pcap_reader.go
        |  Reads PCAP file, extracts packets
        v
Step 3: flow/flow_table.go
        |  Groups packets by 5-tuple (IPs, Ports, Protocol)
        v
Step 4: features/flow_features.go + stats.go
        |  Extracts: Duration, Packet Rate, Byte Rate, IAT Variance, Burstiness
        v
Step 5: baseline/baseline.go + builder.go
        |  Calculates MEAN of features for TCP and UDP separately
        v
Step 6: fidelity/fidelity.go
        |  Score = 100 x e^(-distance)
        v
Step 7: fingerprint/fingerprint.go
        |  Labels: DNS-like, HTTP-like, Anomalous
        v
Step 8: metrics/metrics.go
        |  Aggregates: Total Flows, Avg Fidelity, Low Fidelity
        v
Step 9: api/server.go
        |  REST API at port 8080, sends JSON
        v
Step 10: React Dashboard (App.js)
        |  Displays cards, pie chart, bar chart
        v
OUTPUT: Fidelity Score 0 to 100 + Traffic Labels
